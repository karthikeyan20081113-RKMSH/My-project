About Indian Army

Nation First, Always First

The Indian Army is the land-based branch of the Armed Forces of India. It protects the nation's sovereignty, secures borders, assists during natural disasters, and participates in United Nations peacekeeping missions. The Army is known for bravery, discipline, professionalism, and commitment to national service.
